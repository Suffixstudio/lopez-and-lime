Bottle sizes, so you buy the right thing anywhere: 750ml is the standard bottle, sometimes called a fifth. This is what every count above means. 1.75L is a handle, the big one with the loop on the neck. Common at Costco and warehouse stores. One handle replaces two 750ml bottles. 375ml is a half bottle. Half of a 750ml. Fine for a small event, but usually worse value. Rule of thumb: one 750ml bottle of liquor per 8 guests, one 750ml bottle of wine per 7 guests, and about one beer per guest.
